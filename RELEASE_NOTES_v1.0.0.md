# ReportFlow v1.0.0 — Release Candidate

ReportFlow is an evidence-led workflow for professional research reports. This release candidate packages the research core, evidence system, progressive review, model and method selection, knowledge/data helpers, and reproducible HTML-to-PDF publication checks.

## Included

- Bilingual product documentation (`README.md`, `README.zh-CN.md`)
- Project manifests, state schemas, decision logs, and reusable templates
- Evidence ledger, claim–evidence map, conflict log, and chapter review rules
- Renderer discovery, portable publication runtime guidance, PDF preflight, rasterization, and TOC reconciliation
- Publication smoke and regression workflows
- Editable PPTX and DOCX transformation examples

## Boundaries

PPTX and DOCX are secondary transformations of validated research. They do not replace the research source or human review. Publication CI proves reproducibility in a clean environment; final visual judgment remains a human responsibility.
